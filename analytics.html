Total Institutional Aid Disbursed, 2020 to 2026

Aid disbursed across all programs, in millions of dollars. The institution has roughly doubled its aid budget over seven years.

Finding 1

Breadth beats size

Need-based aid has the second smallest award at $6,500, but with 120 awards it commits more money than any other category and reaches the most students.

Finding 2

Research funding is concentrated

The graduate research fellowship is the largest single award at $22,000, but only twelve are offered, so it is a small share of the overall portfolio.

Finding 3

Steady growth

Aid disbursed has risen every year since 2020 without a single flat or down year, which suggests the commitment is budgeted rather than opportunistic.
