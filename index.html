How to Apply

  1. Complete the institutional aid application through the student portal.
  2. Submit a current transcript showing you meet the minimum GPA for the award.
  3. Provide two letters of recommendation for merit and research awards.
  4. Watch for the award decision roughly six weeks after each deadline.
